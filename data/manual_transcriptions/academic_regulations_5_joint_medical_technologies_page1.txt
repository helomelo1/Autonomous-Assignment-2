Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur
Notification dated 20 January 2022
Subject: Regulations for Joint Programmes in Medical Technologies
1. The regulations for the dual degree and Ph.D. programme in Medical Technologies are attached as Annexure-I.
2. The existing regulations of IIT Jodhpur approved for students registered from July 2019 are applicable for the joint programmes.
3. Minimum CGPA/SGPA requirements for continuation in the programme, continuation of assistantship/fellowship, and graduation:

Programme: Masters in Medical Technologies
- Continuation in programme: CGPA 5.0.
- Continuation of assistantship/fellowship: SGPA 6.0 (for first semester, SGPA at least 5).
- For graduation: CGPA 5.

Programme: Ph.D. in Medical Technologies
- Continuation in programme: SGPA 6.5 (for first semester, SGPA at least 6).
- Continuation of assistantship/fellowship: SGPA 6.5 (for first semester, SGPA at least 6).
- For graduation: CGPA 7.5 at the completion of course work.

Programme: Masters-Ph.D. in Medical Technologies
- Continuation in programme: SGPA 6.5 (for first semester, SGPA at least 5).
- Continuation of assistantship/fellowship: SGPA 6.5 (for first semester, SGPA at least 5).
- For graduation: CGPA 7.0.
