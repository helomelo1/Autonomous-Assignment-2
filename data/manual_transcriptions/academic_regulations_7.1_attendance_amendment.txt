Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur
Circular dated 08 July 2024
Subject: Amendment in the Attendance Policy of the Institute for the Students
The Senate in its 38th Meeting held on 26th June 2024 vide resolution number 38.2.7 approved an amendment to the existing attendance policy.

Existing policy (superseded): Under the earlier policy, attendance below 75% could affect course registration and examination eligibility. The circular states that this existing policy is superseded by the revised policy below.

Revised policy: Students are expected to have full attendance in each course unless they take leave of absence for valid medical or bona fide reasons. In any case, a student's attendance should not fall below 75% in a course. Attendance records must be made available weekly to students by instructors/TAs. Course instructors/TAs must inform students about the attendance policy at the beginning of the course in the First Course Handout (FCH).

If a student remains absent from a class without sanctioned leave for more than two weeks, the course instructor may recommend de-registration of the student from the course.

If a student is absent from all academic activities for four or more weeks (not necessarily contiguous) in a semester, with or without sanctioned leave, the student's registration will result in automatic semester withdrawal for all courses.
[[PAGE: 2]]
If a student's attendance falls below 75% without any valid reason, the instructor can recommend de-registration from the course to the SUGC/SPGC.

This policy supersedes the earlier policy in its totality.
